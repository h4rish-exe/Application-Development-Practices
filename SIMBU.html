SILAMBARASAN TR.

About:

Silambarasan TR (STR) is a popular Tamil actor, director, singer, and filmmaker. Known for his energetic performances and versatile acting, he has starred in films like Manmadhan, Vinnaithaandi Varuvaayaa, Silambattam, Maanadu, and Vendhu Thanindhathu Kaadu. He has a strong fan following and is known for his unique style and screen presence.

Career:

Simbu started his career as a child artist and later became one of the popular leading actors in Tamil cinema. He has explored different genres, from romance and action to intense and experimental roles.

Acting:

Simbu is known for his versatile acting, dialogue delivery, body language, and emotional performances. Movies like Vinnaithaandi Varuvaayaa, Manmadhan, Maanadu, and Vendhu Thanindhathu Kaadu showcase different sides of his acting.
